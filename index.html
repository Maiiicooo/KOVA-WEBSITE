About KOVA

For the days you want to go somewhere, but don't know where.

The app

A place to swim, a view worth the walk, a quiet corner of the city. The KOVA app brings these spots together on one map, so you can find somewhere to spend your afternoon.

KOVA starts in Belgium. We’re building the map spot by spot before expanding further.

Your spots

Know a spot in worth sharing? Add it in the KOVA app. Each submission is reviewed before it goes live. Once approved, your spot appears on the map for others to discover.

Spot types

Three colours, three types of spots. Each coloured X on the map shows what kind of place you'll find: water, nature or urban.

  • Water spots

    In and around the water.

  • Nature spots

    A little further outside.
